*"אני לא יכול לעלות לאוויר לפני שיש לי סליקה, פרסום, משלוחים. המוצר חייב להימכר, להישלח,
ולהתפרסם."* All three, before launch. This raises `GO_LIVE_CHECKLIST.md` **§5 (shipping) from
"not blocking" to blocking** — it had been marked non-blocking because the flat price works.

**The distinction still worth holding when planning:** a product IS already sold and shipped today —
the buyer picks a method, pays a flat price, the seller ships it. What is missing is the
**automation** (Sendit: label, tracking, pickup). So §5 blocks at the level of "a complete
experience", not "nobody can buy" — that changes how much work it is, not whether it happens.

**Two premises he explicitly rejected, both written into the checklist by a session as if agreed:**

1. *"לחזור לזה כשיש ח.פ + דמו + **מוכרים בהמתנה** — ואז יש גם מיקוח"* → *"שום מוכרים בהמתנה לא יהיו,
   הכל צריך לעבוד חלק לפני השקה"* and *"זו החלטה שלא אני קיבלתי, ה'כוח מיקוח' הזה. ממש לא."*
   The payment integration is **built before launch**. A rate can be improved later; an integration
   cannot be deferred. Removed from §3.
2. That advertising could be a post-launch concern — already reversed on 2026-08-04
   ([[project_ads_block_launch]]), and restated here as one of the three.

**How to apply:** never write a deferral into a planning doc as though he agreed to it. A trade-off a
session invents reads, months later, exactly like a decision he made — and he will act on it. If a
deferral seems right, say it in the conversation and let him decide; if it goes in the doc, the doc
says who decided and when.
