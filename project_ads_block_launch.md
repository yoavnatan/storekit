**Advertising is launch-blocking (owner, 2026-08-04):** *"אני לא מתכוון לעלות לאוויר עם נתוני דמו או כשזה לא מחובר — זה חלק ממה שהאתר מציע."* `GO_LIVE_CHECKLIST.md` §2 was tagged "post-launch, blocks marketing only" until then; it is now 🚀 blocking, and the header carries the reversal plus its cost.

**Why:** advertising is part of what the platform sells a seller, so launching without it sells something that doesn't exist. The cost he accepted: the launch date is now tied to Google/Meta approval speed (Merchant Center, domain verification, an active ads account), which can only start **after** the domain is live — that was the original reason it sat outside the blocking path.

**The mitigation that got dropped:** "hide the seller advertising tab / mark it בקרוב until §2.5 passes" (decided 2026-07-30, never built). It was the way to launch early without showing a seller demo numbers. With §2 blocking, a real seller and an unconnected tab never coexist, so it is moot — marked as such in §2.5 and removed from the "doable today" list.

**Ordering he restated, and it is the point of §2.5:** verify first, sell later. No seller pays for a boost until all five layers pass; layers 1-3 are free, and layer 4 (the only one that settles it — our dashboard's impressions/clicks/spend cross-checked against Google Ads / Meta Ads Manager for the same date range) is a small 100-200₪ campaign on **the owner's own money, on his own store**. Sellers never fund the correctness test.

**§2.5 verification is entirely owner-side, one-time — a recurring point of confusion.** Sellers have no Google/Meta account and never will: one GTM, one Pixel, one Merchant Center, one Catalog for the whole platform, which is exactly what makes baseline ads work with zero seller opt-in. "אימות" here means proving to yourself once that the number the dashboard shows a seller is the real number from Google, before charging for a boost — not verifying sellers.

See [[project_ads_verification_plan]] for the layer detail and [[project_business_model_pricing]] for why ad billing is a separate component from commission.
