Delete Product
DELETE
https://sandbox.payme.io/api/products/{guid}

The delete-product endpoint is a critical component of the product management system. It allows clients to remove products from the available products list based on specific identifiers. This operation is essential for maintaining an up-to-date and accurate product catalog, ensuring that obsolete or discontinued items are no longer available in the inventory.
